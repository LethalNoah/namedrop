Namedrop — Privacy Policy

Effective July 23, 2026

Namedrop is a free multiplayer party game playable in a web browser and as a Discord Activity. It is designed to store as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Display name — either a name you type, or (in the Discord Activity, with your consent via Discord's authorization prompt) your Discord display name.
  • Discord user ID and avatar URL — only in the Discord Activity, used to show your avatar and light up your card while you speak.
  • Game state — the room you're in, the characters players assign each other (public Wikipedia article titles and image links), turn and scoring state.

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, payment details, messages, or audio. The speaking indicator uses Discord's start/stop speaking events only — no voice audio is ever received or processed by Namedrop.

Where it lives and how long

Game state is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database. Rooms are automatically deleted roughly 24 hours after creation. Your display name is also remembered in your own browser's local storage so it can be pre-filled next time; you can clear it by clearing site data.

Discord authorization

In the Discord Activity, Namedrop requests the identify and rpc.voice.read scopes to greet you by name and show speaking indicators. The authorization token is used only during your session and is not stored on our servers.

Third parties

Namedrop is hosted on Vercel, stores game state in Google Firebase, and fetches character search results and images from Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation) servers. Each provider processes requests under its own privacy policy. Namedrop contains no advertising and no analytics trackers, and never sells or shares personal data.

Children

Namedrop is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/LethalNoah/namedrop.
